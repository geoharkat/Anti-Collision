About Anti-Collision Analyst

Computes the separation between a design well and its offset wells with the Minimum Curvature Method, searching each offset trajectory continuously, and reports a separation factor.

SF = distance / (hole radii + k × uncertainty radii). Critical and warning thresholds are yours to set.

Limits. The uncertainty radius is a simplified spherical estimate from three MWD terms. It is not an ISCWSA error model: there is no covariance propagation, no tool-code, no magnetic or grid correction and no project-ahead term. Results have not been verified against certified anti-collision software and must not be the basis of drilling decisions.
